Everything You Need to Know About AI Anime Video Generation

What anime styles can Pexo generate?+−

Pexo supports a wide range of anime visual styles, including: Studio Ghibli-inspired (soft watercolor backgrounds, expressive characters, nature-forward atmospheres), Shonen action (high-energy motion, dramatic lighting, dynamic fight choreography), Seinen / dark fantasy (muted palettes, atmospheric tension, detailed worldbuilding), Slice-of-life / romance (warm tones, everyday settings, emotionally nuanced character expression), Cyberpunk / sci-fi anime (neon lighting, futuristic cityscapes, mechanical designs), and Chibi / stylized (simplified character designs with exaggerated expressiveness). Describe the aesthetic you want in your own words — Pexo identifies the right style from your description.

Can I create videos featuring my own original characters?+−

Yes. Pexo supports subject reference inputs — provide a description or reference image of your character, and Pexo maintains consistent character design across every scene in the video. This covers appearance, clothing, and visual identity. For creators building an original IP, series, or visual novel, this means your characters stay recognizable and coherent from the first frame to the last.

Is Pexo an anime art generator as well as a video generator?+−

Yes. Pexo generates both the underlying anime art and the motion video from the same prompt. You do not need to source or create illustrations separately — Pexo handles character design, background art, lighting, and animation as part of a single generation. The output is a video, not a collection of still images you need to assemble yourself.

Can I generate Ghibli-style videos specifically?+−

Yes. Ghibli-style is one of the most requested aesthetics and Pexo generates it well — the characteristic painterly backgrounds, soft natural lighting, flowing movement in environmental details like grass, water, and clouds, and the warm emotional quality of character expression that defines the Ghibli look. Simply describe the scene and specify the Ghibli aesthetic, and Pexo will match it.

Can I get my anime video in different formats for different platforms?+−

Yes. Pexo can generate platform-suited versions of your video — for example, a widescreen version for YouTube and a vertical version for TikTok or Instagram Reels. These are generated to fit each format with appropriate composition and framing, not simply cropped from an existing export. Generating additional format versions uses additional credits.

Do I need to know how to draw, animate, or write prompts?+−

Not at all. Pexo is built for storytellers and creators — not animators or prompt engineers. Describe your scene the way you would describe it to a director: the setting, the characters, what's happening, the mood. Pexo handles the visual translation, the animation, the style, and the output format. If you have a story to tell, Pexo handles everything else.
