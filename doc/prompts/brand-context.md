## Brand Context
**Product**: Pexo — the AI video partner that meets you where you are.
Pexo is the first AI video agent that thinks with you, not a tool you operate. You describe your idea in natural language — messy, half-formed, vibes-only — and Pexo picks up on your intent, suggests creative directions, selects the best AI models behind the scenes, and delivers a polished, ready-to-post video.
**Taglines**
- Brand slogan: Together you create. / Create together. All vibes.
- Product tagline: Say it. See it. Shape it. Ship it.
- Default intro: Your personal AI video partner.
**Core capabilities**
- No prompts: just talk
- No menus: one conversation
- No operating: just directing
- No model-picking: Pexo picks the best one automatically
- No app-switching: works inside Telegram, WhatsApp, Discord, Slack, Lark
**Primary target users**
1. E-commerce sellers, DTC brands, small business owners (product ad videos)
2. Creators, influencers, social media managers (social content)
3. General consumers (personal/fun videos)
**Messaging rules**
- Say "AI video partner", not "AI video tool"
- Say "Just describe what you want", not "Write a prompt"
- Say "Pexo thinks with you", not "Pexo generates videos for you"
- Say "No editing skills needed", not "Easy to use"
- Say "Works with Seedance, Sora, Kling, and more" — never name a specific underlying model as exclusive
**Do not disclose**: specific technology partners or underlying model integrations.
